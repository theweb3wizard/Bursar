How a sale happens

Three steps, no accounts, no keys.

  1. 1
    Agent asks for dataThe API answers “that’ll be $0.01” and hands over a bill number.
  2. 2
    Agent pays on TempoOne stablecoin transfer with the bill number in the memo. Takes about half a second.
  3. 3
    Bursar writes the receiptThe payment is checked against the chain, the bill flips to paid, and it shows up here.

    What competitors see

    Flip the toggle above. This is the whole privacy story.

    The blockchain shows money moving between addresses. That part is public and always will be — anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.

    What it never shows: what was bought, which product earns what, or whose strategy is whose. Every bill number is random. The meaning lives only in your books.

    And when your accountant asks? One click on Export books hands over every invoice, receipt, and timestamp. Hidden from rivals, open to auditors.

    Sell your own API

    Two lines. Your buyers can be any agent, anywhere.

    import { bursarPaywall } from './wrap.js';

app.get('/api/data',
  bursarPaywall({ merchant: YOU, token: pathUSD, price: 10000n }),
  (req, res) => res.json(yourData()));
